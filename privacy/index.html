Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

This policy covers the Brain Arcade games published by Varchas Studios (“we”, “us”) on Google Play: Flip Pairs, Glimpse, Echo, Number Recall, Odd Shade, Block Fit, Color Pour, Tile Trio, Sudoku Calm, Merge Up, Dot Link, Slide Out and Sky Rush, and any future game that links to this page. We are the data controller. You can reach us at thindaam.ai@gmail.com.

Short version: you play without an account. We don’t ask for your name, email or phone number. We don’t collect your location, contacts or advertising ID, we show no ads, and we never sell or share your data. You can delete everything with one tap — see How to delete your data.

1. What we collect

When you open a game for the first time, it creates an anonymous profile for that install. We store:

DataExampleWhy
Anonymous player IDa random code like 3f2a…to tell your scores apart from other players’
Display name“Brave Otter 12” (made up by the app; you can change it)shown next to your scores on leaderboards
Scoresgame, mode (classic/daily), score, level, round length, date and timeleaderboards, daily challenge, your personal best
Gameplay eventseach event has its name (“app opened”, “round started”, “round finished”, “new best”, “shared a result”, “reminder turned on”, “tapped one of our other games”), the game, and the time on your device. Depending on the event it also has: mode, score, level, round length, whether it was a practice round, how many games you’ve played, a few game-specific round stats (for example longest combo, misses or medal), your language setting (when the app opens), your reminder time (only when you turn the daily reminder on) and which of our games you tappedto see which parts of the game are too hard, too easy or broken, and fix them
App version“1.2.0”, sent with your profile and with each scoreto spot bugs that only affect one version

That’s all. We do not collect: advertising IDs, hardware device IDs (such as Android ID or IMEI), precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, email, phone number, payment details, messages, or the list of apps installed on your phone. (The app checks on your phone whether our other games are installed, so it can show “Play” or “Get”; that answer stays on your phone.)

Technical data handled by our hosting provider. When the app connects to our servers, the connection reveals your IP address. Our database provider (Supabase) keeps it in short-lived security logs to prevent abuse such as mass fake sign-ups. We don’t copy it into our game data, don’t use it to work out where you are, and don’t use it for anything else.

Don’t put personal information (like your real name) in your display name — other players can see it.

What stays on your phone.

  • Reminders: if you turn on the daily reminder, it is a local notification scheduled by the app on your phone. We run no push server and send you nothing from our side. Only the reminder time is sent, as a gameplay event (and not at all if gameplay stats are off). Turn it off in Settings or in Android’s notification settings.
  • Android Auto Backup: your progress and settings (including the anonymous player ID) are saved on your phone, and Android may back them up to your own Google account backup so they come back when you reinstall or move to a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own terms; we can’t see it. You can turn it off in Android’s backup settings.

2. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • Running the games — leaderboards, the daily challenge, personal bests. Needed to provide the features you use (performance of a contract, GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); LGPD Art. 7, V).
  • Improving the games — looking at gameplay events in aggregate. Based on our legitimate interest in making the games work well (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); LGPD Art. 7, IX). The data is pseudonymous and minimal. You can switch this off any time under Settings → Share anonymous gameplay stats; scores still work.
  • Security and abuse prevention — rate limits, stopping cheating (legitimate interest).

We don’t use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions that affect you.

3. How long we keep it

  • Gameplay events: deleted automatically after 13 months.
  • Your profile and scores: kept while you play. If you don’t open the game for 24 months, your anonymous profile, display name and scores are deleted automatically.
  • When you delete your data (section 7): removed right away from our live database; backup copies are overwritten within days.

4. Who processes it

We don’t sell or rent your data and don’t share it with anyone for their own purposes. It is handled only by service providers working for us under contract:

  • Supabase, Inc. — hosts our database and anonymous sign-in (servers in Frankfurt, Germany, run on Amazon Web Services). Bound by a Data Processing Agreement.
  • Google — distributes the apps through Google Play. Google’s own processing (downloads, purchases, crash reports you choose to send) is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

Other players see only your display name and scores on leaderboards. We may disclose data if the law requires it, or to a buyer if the games are sold (the buyer must keep this policy’s promises).

5. International transfers

Our data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt). If you play from outside the EU, your data is transferred there. Where our providers access it from other countries (for example Supabase support staff in the United States), the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK Addendum), or an equivalent safeguard required by your local law.

6. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, and you can object to our use of gameplay events (or just turn off the setting). We answer within 30 days and never charge or treat you differently for asking. Because you have no account, we may ask for your display name and which game you play so we can find your data.

  • EU / EEA / UK (GDPR, UK GDPR): you also have the right to restrict processing and to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
  • California and other US states (CCPA/CPRA and similar laws): we do not sell or share personal information (including for cross-context behavioural advertising) and don’t use sensitive personal information. You have the right to know, delete and correct, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights. In the last 12 months we collected the categories “identifiers” (anonymous ID, display name) and “internet or other electronic network activity” (scores, gameplay events, language and reminder settings), for the purposes in section 2.
  • Brazil (LGPD): you can also ask for confirmation that we process your data, anonymisation, information about who we share it with, and you may complain to the ANPD.
  • Other countries (e.g. India, Japan, South Korea, the Gulf states): you have the rights your local law gives you; email us and we’ll help.

7. How to delete your data

This section is also published on its own at vasmikrishna.github.io/varchas-games/delete-data/.

It applies to these Google Play apps from Varchas Studios: Flip Pairs, Glimpse, Echo, Number Recall, Odd Shade, Block Fit, Color Pour, Tile Trio, Sudoku Calm, Merge Up, Dot Link, Slide Out and Sky Rush. You don’t need an account, and you don’t need to reinstall anything.

  • In the app: open the game, go to Settings → Delete my data and confirm. Your anonymous profile, display name, scores and gameplay events for that game are deleted from our servers immediately, and the game’s progress on your phone is cleared. Each game keeps its own profile, so do this in every game you play. The game then starts fresh with a new anonymous profile. If you’re offline, the request is kept and sent as soon as you’re back online.
  • By email (without the app): email thindaam.ai@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, the name of each game and your display name in each game (shown in the game’s Settings). We delete the data within 30 days, confirm by email, and then delete your email too.

What is deleted: anonymous player ID, display name, scores and gameplay events. Nothing is kept, except that our database backups are overwritten within days and our host’s short-lived security logs (IP addresses) expire on their own.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your data from our servers — use one of the options above.

8. Children

The games are intended for adults (18 and over) and are not directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used our games, email thindaam.ai@gmail.com and we’ll delete their data.

9. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). The database is encrypted at rest, and each player can only read their own profile and scores. No system is perfect, but if a breach affects you, we’ll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
