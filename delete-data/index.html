Delete your data

Brain Arcade games by Varchas Studios · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from these Google Play apps, all published by the developer Varchas Studios:

  • Flip Pairs
  • Glimpse
  • Echo
  • Number Recall
  • Odd Shade
  • Block Fit
  • Color Pour
  • Tile Trio
  • Sudoku Calm
  • Merge Up
  • Dot Link
  • Slide Out

You don’t need an account, and you don’t need to reinstall anything.

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open the game.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data and confirm.

Your anonymous profile, display name, scores and gameplay events for that game are deleted from our servers immediately, and the game’s progress on your phone is cleared. Each game keeps its own profile, so do this in every game you play. If you’re offline, the request is kept and sent as soon as you’re back online.

Option 2: by email (without the app)

Email thindaam.ai@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”. Include the name of each game and your display name in each game (shown in the game’s Settings). We delete the data within 30 days, confirm by email, and then delete your email too.

What is deleted

Anonymous player ID, display name, scores and gameplay events. Nothing is kept, except that our database backups are overwritten within days and our host’s short-lived security logs (IP addresses) expire on their own.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your data from our servers — use one of the options above.
